🛡️ ElderShield

Smart Safety Alert System for Elderly People Living Alone

📌 About the Project

ElderShield is a smart safety system designed to help protect elderly people who live alone.

The system detects an unrecognized person entering or approaching the elderly person's home and sends an alert to a trusted family member or close contact.

The trusted person can then call the elderly person directly and check the situation.

🎯 Problem

Many elderly people live alone while their children or close family members live in another city or location. Family members may not always be able to stay with them or continuously monitor their safety.

ElderShield provides a simple way to alert trusted people when an unrecognized person is detected.

💡 Our Approach

Detect → Alert → Call → Human Decision

The system is designed to support communication between elderly people and their trusted contacts rather than replace it.

🚀 Planned Features

- Unrecognized person detection
- Real-time safety alerts
- Trusted contact notification
- Camera-based monitoring
- Simple interface for elderly users
- Emergency support features
- Alert history

🔒 Privacy

The system is designed with elderly users' privacy and safety in mind. Detection and alerts should be limited to the necessary information required for the trusted contact to respond.
